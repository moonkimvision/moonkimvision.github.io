Lead Yu is the twins' dad, the only face in the family that shows everything. Lead No is his coworker from the same hiring class, dragged home after work every single night. Same job title — so whenever something goes wrong, it's a joint responsibility.

Following the twins' rule, each of them gets just one dot of orange: Lead Yu's watch strap and Lead No's lanyard.
